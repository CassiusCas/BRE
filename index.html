Schools

  • E Elementary, circle
  • M Middle, square
  • H High school, diamond
  • + Combined or other grades
  • Blue is public
  • Burgundy is private

The shape is the school’s main level. It still shows under every age group it teaches, so a 7–12 high school appears with both Middle and High. The ring is a combined school. Sixth grade stays elementary when the school stops there.

Amenities

    Named chains keep their own color. Other places in a category share one symbol. Fuel, propane, auto parts, lumber, and fire stations start off.

    Areas

    Each suburb and county has its own color, and the name is drawn in that color. Suburbs are city and town limits, plus census places. County lines are visible and are not clickable. Neighborhoods are a separate layer for Birmingham and the close-in suburbs. A tap opens the neighborhood when that layer is on. Otherwise a tap opens the school district, or the suburb if Districts are off. The dashed ring is 50 miles from downtown Birmingham.
