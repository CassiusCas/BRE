Dave Ramsey's rule for an affordable house: keep your monthly payment at or under 25% of your monthly take-home pay. That 25% limit includes principal, interest, property taxes, homeowners insurance, homeowners association (HOA) fees, and private mortgage insurance (PMI). PMI isn't required when you put at least 20% down.

Typically 2–5% of the mortgage amount. That's about $4,800–12,000 on a $240,000 mortgage. Closing costs cover property taxes, insurance items, and fees from your title company and lender. They vary by area and don't include your real estate agent. Pay them in cash instead of rolling them into the loan.
Line-item estimate for Hoover, Alabama

Categories follow Ramsey's closing-costs guide. Recording taxes are Alabama law: $0.15 per $100 of the mortgage and $0.50 per $500 of deed value above the mortgage recorded with it. Jefferson and Shelby sellers usually buy the owner's title policy; the buyer pays the lender's policy. Choose Itemized above to use this total.

Sources and assumptions

  • 25% rule, 20% down, and closing cost guidance: Ramsey Solutions, "Closing Costs" (updated Oct 2026) and Ramsey's mortgage comparison ($300,000 home, 20% down, 6.5% 15-year, 7% 30-year).
  • Millage: City of Hoover (72.6 mills Jefferson, 66.5 Shelby), Jefferson County 2026 rates, Shelby County rates, and the Alabama Department of Revenue 2025 millage table. Jefferson County adds a $5 storm water fee on homes.
  • Assessment and homestead: Alabama DOR. Class III homes are assessed at 10%. H-1 exempts $4,000 of assessed value from state tax and $2,000 from county tax; school levies still apply.
  • Insurance: Insurance.com 2026 city rates (Hoover $2,414/yr) and U.S. Census 2025 ACS medians via MonitorBankRates.
  • Recording taxes: Alabama recordation tax. Title: Stewart Title Alabama schedule of charges (2026), lender policy $2.50 per $1,000.
  • Inspections: Trammell Inspection Services, Ally Property Inspections, and Sterling Home Inspection published Birmingham prices (2026).
  • Estimates only. Your lender's Loan Estimate and Closing Disclosure are the real numbers.
